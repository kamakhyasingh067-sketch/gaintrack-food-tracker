GAINTRACK INSTALLATION (Android)

This folder is a small installable web app (PWA), not an APK file.

1. Upload the contents of this folder to a static web host that supports HTTPS (for example, Netlify Drop).
2. Open the HTTPS site address on your Android phone in Chrome.
3. Tap Chrome's three-dot menu and choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.
4. Open GainTrack from the new home-screen icon. Once it has loaded once, its app files are cached for offline opening.

IMPORTANT DATA NOTE:
Meal and weight records are stored in the browser/app storage on that phone. Do not clear Chrome/site data or uninstall/reset the app unless you have exported your records. Use Export CSV inside the app for a backup. Data is not automatically synced to another phone.

The food-photo feature stores photos locally; it does not automatically recognize food. Enter the food and portion details yourself.
